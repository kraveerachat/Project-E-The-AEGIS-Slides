# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Owner-requested React, TypeScript, Vite, GSAP, SVG and Lucide icons. Vercel build output is dist.

## Users
Student presenters, their academic audience and reviewers at Suranaree University of Technology. Presenters record a guided presentation of no more than 15 minutes.

## Product Purpose
Explain the AEGIS prototype through exactly 19 scenes, with traceable report content, interactive architecture diagrams, replacement-ready media stages and a bilingual closing scene.

## Operating Context
1920×1080 recording composition; keyboard and button navigation; fullscreen; smaller-screen reading mode. No production connections, authentication, private backend, or external API is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints
IDEA 1 is AEGIS Drive. IDEA 2 is AEGIS Monitor. IDEA 3 is Network and Web Application Security Assessment. The final report supersedes earlier drafts. Assessor and development-team evidence remain separate. The speaking plan is 14:50 including two 90-second demo slots and a 15-second closing scene. Application screenshots and recordings are owner-supplied later.

## Brand Commitments
Three owner-approved images establish warm cream, navy, violet/royal blue, coral and occasional lime, oversized type, asymmetric editorial panels and geometric arcs. Preserve the two supplied original logo files. Decorative generated artwork is not evidence.

## Evidence on Hand
The_AEGIS_Project-Final.pdf (180 pages), read locally and not redistributed. Two owner-supplied original equipment/cabling photographs are included unchanged. Editable section references accompany the content. The report supplies the official title and Thai student names; the owner supplied the exact English spellings and student IDs for this presentation. Internal IP addresses, device hostnames, tokens and private endpoints remain excluded from public content. Real application screenshots/videos are missing.

## Product Principles
- Explain system boundaries before features.
- Keep every factual result traceable to the report.
- Distinguish reported implementation, limitations and planned integration.
- Preserve recording layout when assets are missing.
- Keep presentation controls predictable and motion optional.

## Accessibility and Inclusion
Semantic controls, visible focus, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, media controls and a reflowing reading mode. Thai is the default presentation language; English is immediately selectable and the selection persists when browser storage is available.
